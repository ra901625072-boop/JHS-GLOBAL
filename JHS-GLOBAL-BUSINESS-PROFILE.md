## 7. Commercial Policies & Export Operations

### 7.1 Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ)
* **Standard Bulk Shipments**: 1 × 20ft Full Container Load (FCL), representing approximately 18–24 Metric Tonnes (MT) depending on product density.
* **Specialty Extracts & Actives**: Trial Less-than-Container Load (LCL) shipments accommodated from 100 kg to 500 kg for high-value botanical extracts and cosmetic chemicals.
* **Mixed Container Consolidation**: Multi-product formulators can consolidate multiple items (e.g., Castor Oil drums alongside dry extract drums) into a single 20ft or 40ft container under one unified bill of lading and export document set.

### 7.2 Quotations & Incoterms 2020
* **Quoting Terms**:
  * **FOB (Free on Board)**: Ex-Mundra Port, Hazira Port, or Nhava Sheva (JNPT).
  * **CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight)**: To any major international discharge sea port designated by the buyer.
  * **CFR (Cost & Freight)**: Delivered to foreign discharge port.
  * **EXW (Ex-Works)**: Processing facility Gujarat / warehouse loading point.
* **Quote Turnaround**: 48 business hours from receipt of detailed requirements.
* **Price Validity**: Proforma quotes are valid for 3 to 7 calendar days due to crop harvest fluctuations and ocean freight adjustments.

### 7.3 International Payment & Banking Protocols
1. **Telegraphic Transfer (T/T)**: Standard commercial deposit (typically 30%) with the balance payable against presentation of scanned non-negotiable shipping documents (Bill of Lading, COA, Phytosanitary Certificate).
2. **Letter of Credit (L/C)**: Irrevocable Letter of Credit at Sight issued or confirmed by a prime international commercial bank.

### 7.4 Sample Evaluation Policy
* Pre-shipment evaluation samples (100g to 500g) are provided to qualified commercial buyers for laboratory assay verification and formulation testing.
* Each sample is dispatched with the lot-specific **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** and **Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS/SDS)**.
* Sample product is complimentary; international express courier charges (DHL / FedEx) are either paid by the buyer or credited against the initial FCL commercial order.

### 7.5 Private Label & Contract Packaging (OEM)
* JHS GLOBAL provides private-label retail and foodservice packaging:
  * Heat-sealed stand-up zipper pouches (100g to 1 kg).
  * Food-grade airtight HDPE / PET canisters.
  * Multi-wall master shipper cartons with buyer-specific branding.
  * Multi-lingual regulatory labeling compliant with FDA, EU, or destination country authorities.

## 8. Quality Assurance, Testing & Cargo Protection

### 8.1 Laboratory Testing & Documentation
Every export consignment undergoes mandatory laboratory screening prior to container stuffing:
* **Active Marker Assay**: High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) / UV testing for active constituent verification (e.g., Curcumin %, Withanolides %, Boswellic Acids %).
* **Physical & Chemical Analysis**: Moisture content (Karl Fischer), acid value, bulk density, mesh particle size, and ash content.
* **Safety & Purity Screening**:
  * Heavy Metals screening (Lead, Arsenic, Cadmium, Mercury via ICP-MS).
  * Microbiological profiling (Total Plate Count, Yeast & Mold; negative for E. coli, Salmonella, S. aureus).
  * Pesticide residue screening adhering to destination thresholds (EU MRL / US FDA).
  * Aflatoxin and Ethylene Oxide (ETO) absence testing where applicable.

### 8.2 Third-Party Independent Inspection
Buyers may nominate independent international inspection agencies prior to loading:
* **Approved Agencies**: SGS, Bureau Veritas, Intertek, Cotecna.
* **Inspection Scope**: Pre-shipment random sampling, weight check, laboratory re-testing, container cleanliness audit, and stuffing/seal supervision.

### 8.3 Maritime Moisture & Cargo Protection
To eliminate moisture damage, sweating, and container rain during long ocean voyages:
* All container stuffing occurs under dry, covered warehouse conditions.
* Heavy-duty multi-ply Kraft paper container liners and corrugated floor protection are installed.
* High-absorption calcium chloride desiccant bags (container dry bags) are suspended inside the container.
* Mandatory container pre-checks ensure sound floors, clean walls, and zero roof pinholes or odor contamination.

## 10. Standard Export Documentation Checklist

Every JHS GLOBAL export shipment is accompanied by a complete documentation bundle:
1. **Commercial Invoice**: Detailing HS codes, contract numbers, and Incoterms.
2. **Detailed Packing List**: Net weight, gross weight, pallet counts, and drum/bag marks.
3. **Clean "On Board" Ocean Bill of Lading (B/L)**: 3 original + 3 non-negotiable copies.
4. **Certificate of Origin (COO)**: Issued by the authorized Chamber of Commerce / Export Promotion Council.
5. **Government Phytosanitary Certificate**: Issued by the Directorate of Plant Protection & Quarantine, Ministry of Agriculture.
6. **Certificate of Fumigation**: Professional fumigation treatment (Methyl Bromide or Aluminium Phosphide).
7. **Lot-Specific Certificate of Analysis (COA)**: Technical laboratory batch analysis.
8. **Manufacturer Technical Declarations**: Non-GMO statement, TSE/BSE-free declaration, Allergen status sheet, and Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS).
